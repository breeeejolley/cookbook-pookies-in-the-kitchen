Cauliflower and Chickpeas

  • 1 small head of cauliflower, chopped into 1 inch pieces
  • 1 can of chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ground corriander
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp dry thyme
  • oil for roasting

Quick Pickled Onions

  • 1 red onion
  • 1-2 cloves of garlic
  • zest and juice of 1/2 lime
  • 3 Tbsps or so red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup of corriander, chopped
  • salt

Chipotle Yoghurt Sauce

  • 1-2 chipotle peppers from a can with adobo sauce
  • 1/2 cup of unsweetend yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • Juice and zest of 1/2 lime
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1/4 cup of corriander, chopped

Instructions

Toss cauliflower and chickpeas in spices. Roast in the oven at 200C for about 15-20 mins or until golden. For assembly, 3-4 pitas and lettuce.
