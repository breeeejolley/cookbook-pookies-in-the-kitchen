Shahi Paneer

Ingredients

  • 2 packets of paneer
  • 1 onion
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • small knob of ginger
  • 2-3 Tbsp oil
  • 1 corriander stem, fresh
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 1 black cardamom
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 star anise
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/4 tsp tumeric
  • 1/2 tsp corriander dry
  • 6 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 Tbsp fenugreek
  • ~1.5 cups water
  • 150ml thickened cream
  • 1.2 tsp garam masala
  • ~10 cashews

Instructions

Chop onion, garlic, ginger, fresh corriander.

Dice paneer into cubes and set aside.

Heat oil in pan on medium-high heat, add aromatic spices; cumin seeds, cloves, black cardamom, connamon, bay leaf, star anise. Fry for ~2 minutes until fragrant.

Add onion, garlic, ginger, fresh corriander. Cook, stirring occasionally for ~6-10mins, until onions are brown

Remove aromatic spices with tongs and place aside in bowl

Add 1/4 cup of water to the pan, then use hand blender to blend ingredients into a paste

Add back to heat, add tomato paste and stir. Add back in aromatic spices that were removed prior to blending.

Add remaining spices; salt, chilli powder, tumeric, garam masala, corriander dry, sugar, fenugreek, and a small splash of cream. Stir.

Add 1-1.5 cups of water and stir

Allow bubbles to start forming, then reduce heat to low-medium and simmer for ~10-15 mins to thicken curry.

Add remaining cream and paneer. Stir and remove from heat.

(optional) add chopped raw cashews.
