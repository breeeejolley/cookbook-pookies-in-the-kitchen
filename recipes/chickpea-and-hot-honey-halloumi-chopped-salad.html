Chickpea and Hot Honey Halloumi Chopped Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 can of chickpeas
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for halloumi
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp onion salt
  • 1 block halloumi
  • 1 tbsp hot honey (Or just add some chilli flakes and regular honey)
  • 1 yellow peach, (Or an apple or something similar if not in season)
  • 1 cup chopped cherry or grape tomatoes
  • 1 avocado, diced

Dressing

  • 4 spring onion stalks, finely sliced
  • 1/4 cup roughly chopped mint
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 1/2 tsp sumac
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar

Instructions

Preheat oven to 200C. Line a baking sheet and add the chickpeas along with olive oil, cumin and onion salt. Toss until chickpeas are evenly coated and then bake for 30-35 minutes or until crispy and golden.

Prepare the hot honey halloumi by heating up a drizzle of olive oil in a large skillet on medium heat. Add the sliced halloumi, drizzle with hot honey, and fry for 2-3 minutes on each side until halloumi is golden brown. Set aside to let cool slightly, and then chop up into bite-sized pieces.

While chickpeas are roasting, prepare the dressing. Add sliced spring onions, chopped mint, chopped garlic, crushed red pepper and sumac to a small bowl. Heat up the olive oil on medium heat until it starts to sizzle, but not smoke. Pour it over the bowl of spring onions and mint, add in the red wine vinegar and miz. Let sit for at least 10 minutes

Combine all when chickpeas are ready.
