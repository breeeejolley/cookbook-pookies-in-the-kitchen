Spicy Kimchi Tofu Bowl

  • Menu
  • Want to Make

Serves 2-3

Tofu Marinade

  • 1 block of firm or extra-firm tofu, cut or ripped into bite sized pieces
  • 2 Tbsp Kimchi Juice
  • 1 Tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 1 Tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 1 Tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1 Tbsp Dark Soy Sauce
  • 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp Kimchi Hot Sauce or Siracha
  • 1 tsp Constarch (to add to marinade later)

Spicy Kimchi Mayo

  • 1/4 cup Mayo
  • 2 Tbsp of chopped Kimchi (we did double this probably)
  • 1 Tbsp Kimchi Hot Sauce or Siracha
  • Zest of lime

Other

  • Cooked rice for bowl
  • Pickled cucumber and radish
  • Edemame
  • Sesame seeds (optional)
  • Fried Shallots (optional)
  • Lime wedges (optional)

Instructions

Boil the tofu in salted water for 5 minutes (instead of pressing). Drain and pat dry.

In a bowl, whisk together the tofu marinade. Add the tofu and let it marinate for 5 to 10 minutes.

Heat a thin layer of neautral oil in a pan on medium heat. Remove the tofu from the marinade (reserve the leftover marinade) and cook ina single layer for 3 to 4 minutes on one side until golden, then flip and cook another 2 minutes.

Lower the heat. Whisk the cornstarch into the reserved marinade, then pour into the pan and cook for 1 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the tofu.

Don't frget to cook your rice and edemame, and pickle those cucumbers and radishes.
