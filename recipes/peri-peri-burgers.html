Peri Peri Sauce / Marinade

  • 1-3 birdseye chillis
  • 1 medium capsicum
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp malt vinegar
  • 2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp oinion powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp white sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp cooking salt

Perinaise

  • 3 tbsp peri peri sauce
  • 1/2 cup whole egg mayonaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream

Burgers

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 chicken breasts or chicken thigh fillets, OR alternatively I use halloumi
  • 4 soft rolls (I like brioche)
  • 2 tomatoes sliced
  • lettuce of choice
  • Anything else you may like...

Instructions

Peri peri sauce: put the peri peri sauce ingredients in a jug and blitz until smooth.

Marinade chicken or halloumi: pour 1/2 cup of the sauce into a bowl or ziploack bag. Add the chicken or halloumi and turn to coat. Marinate for at least 3 hours, or up to 24 hours in the fridge.

Perinaise: mix together the perinaise ingredients. Set aside.

Cook Chicken / Halloumi: Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a fry pan over medium / medium high heat and fry em up! Rest chicken and cover loosely with foil - rest for 5 minutes. Toast the rolls in the meantime.

Then, assemble and enjoy! Use the perinaise sauce and veges to assemble your burger.
