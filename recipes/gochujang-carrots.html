Gochujang Carrots

Ingredients

  • 3 large carrots, each chopped in 2 long halves
  • 50g salted butter
  • 1 heaped Tbsp gochugang
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1.5 cups greek yoghurt
  • 1 cup mint and corriander leaves (washed)
  • 1/2 lime juice
  • olive oil
  • Optional garnish: dill

Instructions

Find your biggest pan and place on super high heat. Add a glug of olive oil and bring to high heat. Add your carrots middle side down first and char hard, you want to almost burn them. Flip over and do the same to the skin side. Once you've achieved a deep char, add in your butter and gochujang and mix together whilst melting into the pan.

Braise your carrots in your spicy butter for 10 minutes on medium low heat. Basting the carrots every now and again. Add your honey over the top, roll your carrots through the sauce and cook for another 5 minutes or until your fork easily pierces through the thickest part of the carrot with no resistance.

Now for the Herb Yoghurt Sauce. This one is easy, mix the yoghurt, leaves and lime juice together. Spread on a large plate, and when you're ready put those carrots on top! Garnish with the left over spicy butter and dill.
