Ingredients

  • Garaetteok (Korean rice cakes) (300g), separated (if you are using frozen or packaged rice cakes, separate them first then soak in warm water for 15 mins before cooking to soften.)
  • 2 fish cake sheets (105g), sliced into medium size pieces
  • 1/2 medium onion (75g), thinly sliced
  • 2 leaves of cabbage (145g), sliced into medium size strips
  • 1 stalk of spring onion, chopped
  • Some cooking oil – I use rice bran oil
  • Some roasted sesame seeds to garnish

Tteobokki Sauce

  • 2 Tbsp Korean chili paste (Gochujang)
  • 2 Tbsp raw sugar or caster sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Korean chili powder (Gochugaru)
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 cup water

Instructions

Pre heat a wok/skillet on medium high heat until well heated. Add some cooking oil and the onion. Stir it until the onion softens. Add the cabbage and stir until it softens.

Add the water, and then add the sauce and stir in gently. Add the fish cakes and the rice cakes and then cover to let boil for 2 minutes.

Reduce the heat to low, and let simmer until the sauce thickens and the rice cakes start to swell in size and are of a soft, squishy texture. Then take off heat.

Serve with garnish of spring onion and sesame seeds.

NOTE: In Korea they often eat this with some cheese! Feel free to garnish with some cheese, a googy boiled egg, kimchi or even some greens. I also like to crumb fry some tofu sometimes for an added texture.
