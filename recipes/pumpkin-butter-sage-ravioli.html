Pumpkin Butter Sage Ravioli

(recipe by Half Baked Harvest)

Ingredients

Serves 6

Butter Sage Sauce

  • 8 Tbsp salted butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup roughly chopped walnuts
  • 16 fresh sage leaves
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar, or white wine (we used apple cider vinegar)
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 pound (0.45kg) of pumpkin ravioli
  • Optional: 1/3 cup manchego cheese, grated (for serving)

Ravioli

  • 210g pumpkin (roasted, mashed)
  • 1 egg
  • 2.5 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt

Pumpkin filling

  • 210g pumpkin (roasted, mashed)
  • 1/4 cup ricotta
  • 1/4 cup shredded gouda cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 Tbsp blue cheese (I just chopped up a small handful)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 egg yolk
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

Prepping

Preheat your oven to 200°C. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper, and chop your pumpkin into rough 4cm pieces. At a reasonable size to roast. Roast until fully soft in the middle and mashable, roughly 20-30mins. You will be including the mashed pumpkin in both the filling and the pasta, so it's a good idea to get this started early.

Making the Ravioli

Line a second baking sheet with parchment paper.

In the bowl of a stand mixer, combine the pumpkin, eggs, flour, and salt. Using the dough hook, knead until the dough forms a ball, adding more flour as needed. Alternately, this can be mixed by hand. Cover and let rest 10-15 minutes.

To make the filling. Combine all ingredients in a food processor until evenly mixed. Season with salt and pepper.

Dump the dough out onto a floured surface and cut into quarters. Roll the pasta dough into lasagna sheets, or alternatively use a pasta machine to make smooth sheets. Place 2 teaspoons of filling one inch apart over half the pasta sheets. Brush around the filling with water to moisten and then fold the sheet over the filling, pressing down to seal. Cut into squares. Be sure to keep the ravioli covered as you work to prevent them from drying out. Repeat with remaining dough and filling. Transfer the ravioli onto the prepared baking sheet. If not cooking right away, cover, and refrigerate for up to 4 hours or freeze.

Drop the ravioli into the boiling water and cook until al dente, 3-4 minutes. Drain.

Making the Butter Sage Sauce

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Boil the ravioli according to package directions, 3-4 minutes. Drain.

Meanwhile, melt together the butter, garlic, walnuts, and sage in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook, stirring occasionally until the butter is browning and the sage is crisp, 4-5 minutes. Remove the sage leaves from the butter and set aside. Pour the sauce over the cooked ravioli and garnish with the crispy sage leaves.

Reduce the heat to low, add the apple cider or wine, apple cider vinegar, and season the sauce with nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Simmer 1 minute, then drop the ravioli into the butter sauce, gently tossing to combine. Remove from the heat.

Serve the ravioli and butter sauce with the crispy sage and a sprinkle of manchego.s
