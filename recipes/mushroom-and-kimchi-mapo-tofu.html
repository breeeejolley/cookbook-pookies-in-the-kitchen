Mushroom and Kimchi Mapo Tofu

(recipe by Ottolenghi.. I think)

Ingredients

  • 300g mixed mushrooms (a mixture of chestnut, shitake and oyster, for example), caps and stalks roughly broken into 3-4 pieces
  • 2 tbsp peanut (or sunflower oil)
  • 6 spring onions finely sliced, white and green parts kept sperate
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 20g ginger, peeled and finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 3tbsp doubanjiang (aka chilli bean sauce)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 300ml vegetable stock (or water)
  • 1 tbsp potato flour (or cornflour), dissolved in 2 tbsp of water
  • 150g kimchi, roughly chopped
  • 800g soft tofu (not the silken variety), cut into roughly 3cm squares
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp sichuan peppercorns, toasted and roughly crushed
  • steamed rice, to serve

Instructions

This recipe serves 4-6.

Put the pieces of mushroom into a food processor and pulse about 10 times, until finely chopped.

Put the oil into a large non-stick saute pan and place on a medium heat. Add the white parts of the spring onions, along with the garlic and ginger. Cook for 2-3 minutes, until softened, then add all the chopped mushrooms. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook for 8-9 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms have softened and most of the moisture has evaporated.

Add the wine, doubanjiang, and the light and dark soy sauces. Bring to a simmer, then lower the heat and cook for a minute, stirring to combine. Add the stock, then the potato flour mixture, and simmer for a minute until slightly thickened. Add the kimchi, then gently fold in the tofu to warm through.

Transfer to a large serving bowl, drizzle over the sesame oil and sprinke with the Sichuan peppercorns and the reserved green parts of the spring onions.
